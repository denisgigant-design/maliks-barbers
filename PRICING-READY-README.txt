MALIK'S BARBERS - PRICING VIDEO: 2-FILE UPLOAD

These files are ready for the existing GitHub Pages site:
https://github.com/denisgigant-design/maliks-barbers

1. Unzip the archive.
2. Upload both files together to the ROOT of your repository:
    panel-presets.js  (replace the old file)
    pricing-barber-transparent.webm (new video)
3. Commit. Open your barbers website and tap Pricing.

NO HTML EDIT IS REQUIRED. The existing index.html already loads
panel-presets.js. This bundle retains the current published desktop/mobile
wall layouts and panel positions exactly as they appear in the repository.

The original supplied video is approximately one second long. The barber
appears when the Pricing pop-up opens, plays the clip once, and holds his
last pose until you close the card / leave Pricing. A natural floor shadow
and gentle lighting adjustment are included. There is no black background,
no GIF, no paid plugin and no external video service.

The video is transparent VP9 WebM, suited to Chrome on Android and modern
Chromium browsers. Safari transparent WebM support varies by version.

Important: Replacing panel-presets.js with a new exported preset in the future
also removes the embedded animation code. The separate pricing-barber.js
is supplied in the alternative integration ZIP if you later want to move
the effect into its own file.
