MOVEABLE CUSTOMIZATION DIALOGS
- Open Panels, Cards or Lighting.
- Use the new ◀ / ▶ arrows in the dialog header to dock the editor left/right.
- Drag its title/header horizontally to position anywhere between the two sides.
- The editor's horizontal position is remembered privately on that device.
- Dragging the editor does NOT modify the published customer-facing panel layout.

TRANSPARENT VIDEO / iPHONE COMPATIBILITY
- On compatible non-Apple browsers, the actual transparent VP9 WebM plays.
- On Apple WebKit (Safari and Chrome on iPhone/iPad; macOS Safari), an H.264 MP4
  contains the colour image in the left half and its grayscale alpha mask on the
  right. A small WebGL shader restores the actual transparency at playback time.
- The MP4 is conventional H.264/yuv420p with a fast-start atom, muted and
  playsinline. If the browser blocks playback or WebGL is unavailable, a
  transparent still PNG remains visible instead of a black rectangle.
- The separate site's walkthrough.mp4/mobile-tour.mp4 use their existing iOS
  initialization in ios-compat.js (muted/playsinline + first-touch unlock).
- The generated fallback MP4, source WebM and poster were inspected. Actual
  physical Safari/Chrome iPhone playback was NOT tested in this environment;
  after uploading, please verify once on a real iPhone.
